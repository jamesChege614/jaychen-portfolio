How Agentic AI Will Change App Development and Growth in the Next 12 Months — and Why I’m a Strong Fit to Help RevenueCat Lead It

By James Chege · LinkedIn · Portfolio

Over the next 12 months, agentic AI will change app development and growth in a very practical way: it will reduce the distance between idea, execution, iteration, and scale.

Today, a solo builder or a small product team still spends a lot of time stitching tools together, researching workflows, producing technical content, testing acquisition channels, reviewing customer patterns, and refining product decisions. Agentic AI will increasingly sit inside that loop, not just as a chatbot, but as a working layer that can plan, execute, observe, and improve outcomes across development and growth activities.

I believe we are moving into a phase where AI systems will help builders:

  • turn requirements into working prototypes faster
  • connect APIs and services with less manual effort
  • generate technical documentation and implementation guidance more quickly
  • support onboarding and product adoption through better automation
  • run structured growth experiments more consistently
  • surface product friction earlier through usage patterns and feedback loops
  • reduce repetitive operational work so humans can focus on higher-value decisions

This does not mean human judgment becomes less important. It becomes more important. The teams that will win are the ones that know how to combine AI-driven execution with strong product thinking, security awareness, clear communication, and disciplined iteration.

That is one reason I’m interested in this opportunity.

My background sits at the intersection of infrastructure, automation, system reliability, and practical AI-enabled workflows. I have spent the last several years building and supporting secure systems, automating operational processes, integrating platforms through APIs and scripts, and improving how users interact with technology in real environments.

More recently, I have been applying that same thinking to AI and automation work that creates direct business value.

Selected Work

I built a secure backup visibility platform that gave clients real-time status of their database and CCTV backups stored in Amazon S3. It included permission-based downloads and admin controls, and reduced support dependency for backup status checks and data access by about 90%.

I built an AI-assisted job matching platform that fetched open opportunities, filtered them against a client’s qualifications, and highlighted strong matches together with areas for improvement.

I architected and implemented an n8n and agentic AI-based analytics platform integrated with Microsoft Dynamics NAV, with cloud setup, API security, data security, and operational troubleshooting. The solution gave users a more digestible reporting experience and reduced reliance on separate Power BI licensing.

I also built a tender discovery and proposal automation workflow that identified relevant tenders, notified the client, and supported AI-assisted proposal drafting with review and approval before submission.

Across these projects, the pattern is consistent: I focus on building secure, useful, automation-driven systems that reduce manual work, improve clarity, and create measurable value.

Why This Matters for RevenueCat

Because the next generation of builders will not just be traditional app developers. Many will be highly automated creators, AI-assisted operators, and small teams using agents to build, launch, iterate, and grow products faster than before.

I believe RevenueCat is in a strong position to serve that future.

The companies that win in this next phase will not only provide APIs. They will help AI-assisted builders understand how to use those APIs effectively, safely, and creatively.

That is where I believe I can contribute.
